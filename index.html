What We Do
FULL AUTO BODY
SERVICES

From minor scratches to major collision repair, we handle it all. Foreign, domestic, classic — every vehicle treated with the same expert care.

Collision Repair
Full structural and cosmetic repair after any accident — from minor fender benders to major front or rear-end collisions. We restore your vehicle to pre-accident condition.
Auto Painting
Computer-matched paint for a flawless, factory finish. Spot painting, panel painting, and full vehicle repaints. OEM color matching guaranteed.
Frame & Structural
State-of-the-art frame straightening and unibody repair. We use precision measuring equipment to restore factory alignment and structural integrity.
Dent Removal
Paintless dent repair (PDR) for hail damage, door dings, and minor dents. Preserve your original paint while restoring the panel to perfection.
Glass Replacement
Windshield chip repair and full replacement. Side windows, rear glass, and sunroof panels. OEM and OEM-equivalent glass options available.
Insurance Claims
We work directly with all major insurance companies so you don't have to. We handle the paperwork and communication — just bring your vehicle in.
Detailing & Polish
Interior and exterior detailing, paint correction, and ceramic coating. Keep your vehicle looking showroom-fresh after every repair.
Foreign Vehicles
Specialized knowledge and OEM-compatible parts for BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Toyota, Honda, Lexus, Volkswagen, and all foreign brands.
Classic & Specialty
Restoration work for classic and collectible vehicles. Custom metalwork, period-correct paint, and specialty finishes handled with extra care.
